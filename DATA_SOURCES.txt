DATA SOURCES AND RIGHTS

All observations concern plant accessions, not human participants.

Arabidopsis nucleotide calls and published phenotypes:
AtPolyDB, call_method_75 TAIR9 release, Gregor Mendel Institute.
https://github.com/Gregor-Mendel-Institute/atpolydb
214,051 SNPs x 1,307 accessions, A=0 C=1 G=2 T=3.
Phenotype 8_Seed Dormancy: Atwell et al. (2010), DOI 10.1038/nature08800.
Other phenotype columns include 279_DSDS50 and FT10/FT16/FT22.
Original full CSV and accession info are included so rebuilds do not depend
on a future availability of the source link. accession metadata sorted by ID
must NEVER be treated as genotype column order; the second CSV header is
canonical. The source repository carries a CC0-1.0 dedication for the Work,
including its database contents:
https://github.com/Gregor-Mendel-Institute/atpolydb/blob/master/LICENSE
The dedication page and repository terms were reviewed on 2026-11-19.
CC0 does not require attribution, but scientific use should cite Atwell et al.
(2010) and Horton et al. (2012).

External chilling-study phenotypes:
Martinez-Berdeja et al. (2020), DOI 10.1073/pnas.1912451117.
Dryad data DOI https://doi.org/10.25338/B8VS4P
Included original arabidopsis_germ_phen_chilling_DATA.xls.
The Dryad dataset record licenses the deposit under CC0-1.0:
https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.25338/B8VS4P
That rights statement was reviewed on 2026-11-19. Scientific use should cite
the associated PNAS article and the dataset DOI.

Gene coordinates:
TAIR10/Araport11 source-derived genes_tair10.csv, retained for preprocessing.
https://www.arabidopsis.org/
TAIR licenses files in its Public_Data_Releases tree under CC-BY-4.0:
https://www.arabidopsis.org/download_files/Public_Data_Releases/README.txt
That license statement was reviewed on 2026-11-19. Attribution to TAIR and
Phoenix Bioinformatics is required.
The original TAIR9 and TAIR10 nuclear assembly coordinates are compatible;
no release-to-release liftover is required. Gene models remain annotation-specific.

Rice genotype and phenotype source data:
Spindel et al. (2015), PLOS Genetics, DOI 10.1371/journal.pgen.1004982.
Dryad DOI https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.7369p
Public source mirror:
https://www.ricediversity.org/data/Spindel_2015_PLOS/GS_datasets/
CC0-1.0, verified in revision/data/rice_datacite_dryad.json rightsList.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode
Included binned genotype source, raw .75-call-rate HapMap and corrected plot
phenotypes. The numeric dataset discards source line-mean genotype fills and
restores raw missingness. Rice short source-schema quotations are attributed
CC-BY article excerpts, not CC0 data or software MIT; source DOI is retained.

Processed arrays and result tables derive from the sources above under the
terms stated for each source. This package licenses original software under
MIT and creates no broader grant for third-party data or derived arrays.
